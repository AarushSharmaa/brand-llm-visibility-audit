GEO diagnosis cross-model

Notion's visibility is model-dependent — strong on Gemini (83%) but only average on ChatGPT (50%). That gap is the real signal. Both models agree on one thing: Notion doesn't surface for "best-in-class" queries, where Obsidian consistently wins the top slot on both platforms.

The pattern across models points to a positioning problem, not a content volume problem. Gemini surfaces Notion in competitive and comparison contexts; ChatGPT skips it there and reaches for Confluence instead. This suggests ChatGPT's training data skews Notion toward personal use while framing enterprise productivity around Microsoft/Atlassian tools.

The one action that closes both gaps: publish a dedicated "Notion for teams vs. Confluence" comparison page, and a separate "Notion vs. Obsidian for personal knowledge management" piece. These two pages directly target the query types where both models currently fail to surface Notion — and head-to-head content is among the highest-weighted content types in LLM training corpora.
